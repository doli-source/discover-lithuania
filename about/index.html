About

Niv Shimoni

I'm an entrepreneur and AI builder based in Israel. I've been visiting Lithuania regularly since 2017 — dozens of cities, towns, and villages across the country.

I started keeping notes about places I liked: a particular sourdough bakery, a coffee roaster hidden in a courtyard, a restaurant where locals actually eat. Eventually those notes became this site.

Everything on Discover Lithuania is a place I've been to, or one I'd vouch for based on people I trust.

Discover Lithuania now covers over 160 places across Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Trakai, the Curonian Spit, and beyond — cafés, restaurants, nature spots, stays, and ready-made itineraries.
