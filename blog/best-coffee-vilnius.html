7 Best Coffee Shops in Vilnius (2026 Local Guide)

✍️ By Niv Shimoni · 📅 Updated July 2026 · ⏱ 4 min read

Vilnius has quietly become one of the best coffee cities in the Baltics. The scene here isn't about chains — it's specialty roasters, tiny espresso bars, and cafés that take their craft seriously. These are the spots worth your time, picked from dozens of visits across the city.

☕
Espresinė
★★★★★ 4.9 (399 reviews) €5–10

Don't let the size fool you. This tiny espresso bar is one of the most respected coffee spots in Vilnius — locals know it, tourists rarely find it, and the coffee is seriously good. No frills, no fuss. Just exceptional espresso in a space barely big enough to turn around in.

🚀
RocketBean Kavinė
★★★★★ 4.9 (275 reviews) €5–10

A Baltic specialty roastery that started in Riga and expanded to Vilnius — and for good reason. Three types of espresso, excellent filter options, and a consistency that's hard to match. Order the house espresso first and work your way through the menu from there.

BREW. Specialty Coffee
★★★★½ 4.6 (333 reviews) €5–10

The perfect morning stop. BREW combines a serious specialty roaster with excellent pastries — the kind of place you plan to stay 20 minutes and end up lingering for an hour. Get there early; the good pastries go fast.

Backstage Roastery Café
★★★★½ 4.5 (911 reviews) €10–15

One of the best brunch spots in Vilnius — always packed on weekends, always worth the wait. Backstage roasts their own beans in-house, and it shows. The brunch menu goes well beyond what you'd expect from a roastery café. Come for the coffee, stay for the food.

Backstage Cafe Vokiečių str.
★★★★ 4.1 (618 reviews) €5–15

Backstage's second location on Vokiečių Street — founded by photographers, which explains the exposed-brick aesthetic and unhurried atmosphere. Same freshly roasted beans as the HQ, with a brunch menu that rewards a slow morning. Better for a quiet weekday visit than the main branch.

🖤
StrangeLove
★★★★½ 4.5 (1,375 reviews) €5–10

One of Vilnius's original specialty roasters — open since 2015 and still going strong. They roast their own beans and have a large terrace alongside the Bernardine Gardens. The most popular café in the city for good reason: great coffee, great location, reliable every time.

🎪
Coffee Circus Piano
★★★★½ 4.7 (483 reviews) €5–10

A specialty coffee community built around a big red piano at its center. Customers play, baristas talk about coffee, and nobody is in a hurry. It sounds gimmicky until you're there — then it makes perfect sense. One of the most characterful spots in the Old Town.

Quick tips for coffee in Vilnius

  • Most specialty cafés open at 8–9am and close by 6–7pm
  • Cash is accepted everywhere, but card is fine too
  • A flat white or cortado runs €3–4; filter coffee around €3
  • Weekday mornings are the best time — weekend brunches fill up fast
  • All spots listed here are in or near the Old Town — easily walkable
