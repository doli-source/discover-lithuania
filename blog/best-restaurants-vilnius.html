8 Best Restaurants in Vilnius (2026 Local Guide)

✍️ By Niv Shimoni · 📅 Updated July 2026 · ⏱ 5 min read

Vilnius punches well above its weight when it comes to food. Georgian, Peruvian, Jewish Litvak, Italian, Tex-Mex — the city's restaurant scene is genuinely diverse and surprisingly affordable. These are the places worth your time, picked from dozens of meals across the city.

🍝
Le Travi
Italian
★★★★½ 4.6 (536 reviews) €20–45

A cozy Italian bistro with great pasta and an intimate atmosphere that's hard to find in the Old Town. Proper Italian cooking without the tourist markup — the kind of place you return to every trip and it never lets you down.

🌮
Donde
Tex-Mex
★★★★★ 4.7 (877 reviews) €10–15

Vilnius's go-to Tex-Mex spot — and the tacos are genuinely excellent. Homemade tortillas, proper fillings, and the kind of casual energy that makes you want to order another round. No pretension, just consistently good food at a fair price.

🍚
OSH Halal
Uzbek · Halal
★★★★★ 5.0 €5–10

A one-man operation on Kalvarijų Street where the owner cooks authentic Uzbek plov right in front of you. Three things on the menu, each one done perfectly — the plov is light, flavourful, and nothing like what you'd expect to find in Lithuania. Halal certified. One of Vilnius's most surprising hidden gems.

2 virėjai
Lithuanian
★★★★★ 5.0 (75 reviews) €10–15

A tiny lunch spot run by two local cooks — perfect rating, no tourists, honest Lithuanian food. Exactly what you want in the middle of a Vilnius day. The kind of place that fills up by noon and doesn't advertise because it doesn't need to.

Focacceria
Italian
★★★★★ 5.0 (52 reviews) €5–10

A tiny Italian focacceria that opened in 2025 and quickly earned a perfect rating. Fresh focaccia sandwiches, simple and executed perfectly. Small menu, zero fuss — the kind of spot you stumble on and immediately tell your friends about.

🥣
Baleboste
Jewish Lithuanian
★★★★½ 4.5 (410 reviews) €10–15

Traditional Litvak Jewish cuisine at the foot of the Hales Market — forshmak, borscht, pastrami bagels, and cabbage rolls the way old Vilna used to make them. Vilnius was once called "the Jerusalem of Lithuania," and Baleboste keeps that culinary memory alive. A genuinely unique dining experience you won't find anywhere else.

🌊
Tempo V39
Mediterranean
★★★★½ 4.7 (230 reviews) €25–50

A Mediterranean bistro on Vilniaus Street born from late-night vinyl sessions — natural wines, sharing plates, and an evening crowd that stays for the vibe as much as the food. Come for dinner, plan to linger.

Meat SteakHouse
Steak
★★★★ 4.4 (1,570 reviews) €50+

A serious steakhouse with a dedicated aging chamber — every cut dry-aged for at least 28 days before it reaches your plate. No kids after 5pm. This is for people who take steak seriously, and it delivers.

Quick tips for eating in Vilnius

  • Book in advance for Pachamama and Meat SteakHouse — they fill up fast
  • Lunch is the best value: most restaurants offer set menus for €8–12
  • The Old Town concentrates most of these spots — all walkable
  • Tipping: 10% is standard if you're happy with the service
  • Paupio Turgus food market (Saturday morning) is unmissable for a casual bite
