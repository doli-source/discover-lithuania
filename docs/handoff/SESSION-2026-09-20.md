# Handoff — lithuaniadiscovery.com, 20 September 2026

Written for whoever picks this up next. It covers what the site is, what was
wrong, what changed, what was measured, and the questions still open. Read the
**Open questions** section last but treat it as the point of the document — the
rest is context for it.

19 commits, 431 files, all deployed and verified live.

## 1. What the site is

A Lithuania travel guide, English and Hebrew, run by one person (Niv Shimoni).
169 published places across 10 regions, 5 ready-made routes, 5 blog posts.

| | |
|---|---|
| Domain | lithuaniadiscovery.com |
| Hosting | **GitHub Pages**, behind Cloudflare — *not* Netlify |
| Repo | `doli-source/discover-lithuania`, branch `main` |
| Data | Supabase (`hsovwydscmwyyvsemudg`) — places, translations, regions, dishes, facts, itineraries |
| Front end | Static HTML shells + React/JSX compiled in the browser by Babel |
| Analytics | GA4 property 543452903 · Search Console `sc-domain:lithuaniadiscovery.com` |

**The hosting detail matters more than it looks.** The repo contains
`netlify.toml`, `_headers` and a `.netlify/` directory, and a Netlify site is
still live at `discover-lithuania.netlify.app`. None of it serves the domain.
GitHub Pages ignores both config files, so every rule in them is inert. Anything
that must arrive as an HTTP header is a Cloudflare rule instead.

## 2. Where the traffic actually comes from

This reframes everything and was got wrong twice during the session before the
data settled it.

**The audience is overwhelmingly inside Lithuania, searching in English.**

| Query | Impressions | Position | Country |
|---|---|---|---|
| best restaurants for dinner | 458 | 13.3 | **100% Lithuania** |
| focaccia near me | 159 | 5.1 | **100% Lithuania** |
| best restaurants near me | 82 | 11.0 | **100% Lithuania** |
| best coffee shops nearby | 72 | 12.1 | **100% Lithuania** |
| where to eat in vilnius | 49 | 69.1 | 90% United Kingdom |

The "near me" queries were dismissed mid-session as geographic noise — someone
in Manchester searching for focaccia. They are not. Every one of those 826
impressions is from inside Lithuania: people standing in the country looking for
somewhere to eat. That is the single largest untapped block on the site, and it
converts at roughly one click.

Conversely, a "Nordic opportunity" was announced at 109 impressions and turned
out to be 31 once split by country — the rest was scattered across Brazil,
India and Indonesia, which is noise.

**Lesson for the next person: never quote an impression count without splitting
it by country first.** Both mistakes were avoidable from data already available.

### Hebrew is small

73 Hebrew queries, ~249 impressions in 180 days. Best positions are narrow and
specific — `בית חבד וילנה` at 7.6, `אוכל ליטאי` at 8.5. The broad head terms
(`ליטא`, `וילנה`) sit at position 51–68, behind lametayel.co.il, masa.co.il,
gotravel.co.il and vilnius.co.il — an exact-match domain dedicated to Vilnius
for Israeli travellers.

Hebrew matters to the owner and Israel is the #1 country by clicks (63 of 146),
but the ceiling on Hebrew search volume for this subject is low. Say so plainly
rather than implying head terms are reachable.

## 3. What was broken, and what fixing it revealed

### 15 commits had never been deployed
All prior SEO work was sitting on a local branch. The live site had none of it.
Everything below was built on top of finally shipping that.

### The place count was wrong everywhere
Meta tags across 23 files said 175 places. The rendered page said 169. 175 was
arrived at by counting directories in `/places/`, which had drifted from the
data. **Never derive the count from the filesystem** — it is
`published Supabase rows + the inline `PLACES.push()` entries in
`supabase-data.js``, and that second source is easy to miss (it is worth three
places and caused a wrong answer during this session).

### Hebrew was being indexed as English
`?lang=he` swapped body text client-side but left `<title>`, `<meta
description>`, Open Graph and schema in English. Google indexed the Hebrew URL
as an English page with some Hebrew in it. **This is the mechanical reason
Hebrew sat at position 50–78 while the same pages ranked 6–11 in English** — not
authority, not links.

### 3.3 MB of base64 on every page
`region-images.js` held ten JPEGs as base64 and loaded on every page — 97% of
the site's 2.6 MB transfer, downloaded in full before a visitor saw any of them.
`screens.jsx` already had a fallback to real files, whose path was relative and
404'd. Homepage transfer went 2,615 KB → 786 KB, FCP 1,608 ms → 1,112 ms.

### A canonical pointing at a blocked URL
`app.jsx` declared `/?place=<id>` as canonical while a pending robots.txt commit
would have disallowed that exact pattern — the canonical target would have become
uncrawlable. Caught before deploy.

### Schema that silently counted for nothing
`places/doyou-place/` had an unescaped quote in its JSON-LD, so the whole block
failed to parse — business type, rating, coordinates, breadcrumb, all discarded
by Google, for months, invisibly.

### Two dead region pages
`/explore/utena/` and `/explore/kretinga/` are regions that were merged away.
The pages stayed live and indexable and the SPA cannot render a region absent
from the data, so they were empty results in the index. Now noindex + redirect.

### A leaked credential
The git remote contained a GitHub personal access token in plaintext in
`.git/config`. Removed, moved to macOS Keychain via `gh auth login`. **The token
was exposed in conversation and must be revoked if it has not been.**

## 4. What was built

### Bilingual structure — 190 Hebrew pages
Every indexable page now exists twice: `/thing/` and `/thing/he/`, both with
Hebrew title, description, Open Graph and `inLanguage` in the served HTML.
hreflang declares the pair in the page and in the sitemap. `?lang=he` still
works for visitors and canonicals to the `/he/` page.

All Hebrew content already existed in Supabase and in `data-he` attributes.
**No copy was written.**

- `generate-place-pages.js` — both languages, deletes pages for removed places,
  `--only=<slug>`, `--all`, `--dry-run`
- `generate-section-pages.js` — 16 region/section shells, both languages
- `generate-article-pages.js` — 5 blog posts, swaps `data-he` into the HTML and
  translates the JSON-LD

### Titles aimed at real queries
16 section pages, both languages. Each entry in `generate-section-pages.js`
carries the query and impression count it targets.

```
Vilnius       "Things to Do & See"   → "Where to Eat, Drink & What to See"
Trakai        "Travel Guide"         → "Trakai Island Castle — Day Trip"
Druskininkai  "Spa & Forests"        → "Spa Resorts, Snow Arena & Aquapark"
```

### FAQ — visible + schema, on 19 pages
Questions come from `faq-queries.json` (424 real Search Console queries across
127 pages), classified by intent and asked back in the searcher's terms. Answers
are computed from site data: counts by category, top-rated places with ratings,
observed price ranges, matching route and its length.

Questions travellers do ask but the data cannot answer — **is it safe, when is
the best time to visit, is it expensive overall** — are deliberately absent, and
`check.js` fails if an answer to one appears. Inventing those would be worse
than silence: an answer engine would quote it.

### Security headers
Four headers live via a Cloudflare Response Header Transform Rule named
`security-headers`. Not in `_headers` — that file is inert here.

### `check.js` — 14 rules
Run before every push. Every rule exists because that invariant was already
broken in production: place count, missing/orphan pages, Hebrew pair integrity,
JSON-LD parses, canonical vs robots, region images, inert config, immutable
caching, header conflicts, sitemap, unpushed commits, spelling override, FAQ
integrity, dead regions.

## 6. Open questions — the actual brief

### Needs the owner, not an agent
- **Revoke the exposed GitHub token** if not already done.
- Fix in Supabase, then delete the override in `supabase-data.js`:
  ```sql
  UPDATE regions SET name_he='דרוסקינינקאי' WHERE id='druskininkai';
  UPDATE regions SET name_he='טרקאי'        WHERE id='trakai';
  UPDATE itineraries SET duration='3day'    WHERE id='coast-3day';
  ```
  The Hebrew spellings in the database differ from how people actually search.
  `check.js` warns until the rows are corrected.
- Six places have no coordinates in the data, so no `GeoCoordinates` schema:
  `gravelindos`, `jewish-heritage-tours-kaunas`, `jewish-heritage-tours-vilnius`,
  `musangas`, `private-baltics`, `zvirblio-puota`. Two of them (Musangas,
  Žvirblio puota) are at Liepų g. 64, Klaipėda per their own descriptions.
- The Supabase `places` table has **no street address column**. 235 impressions
  search a street name directly (`backstage cafe vokieciu g`). Adding one would
  let the schema carry a real `PostalAddress`.

### Worth deeper research
1. **Why does `/blog/best-restaurants-vilnius` sit at position 13 for a query
   with 458 impressions, all from inside Lithuania?** Page-one entry there is
   the largest single win available. What do the pages above it have?
2. **Three Backstage branches split 1,260 impressions at positions 24–53 and
   convert none.** Titles were differentiated this session; if that does not
   move them, the problem is authority and the question becomes whether three
   thin pages should be one.
3. **Branded restaurant queries at position 6–11 get zero clicks** —
   `tempo v39` (816 impressions, 1 click). Working hypothesis: the map pack and
   the business's own listing satisfy the searcher before the organic result.
   If true this is structural and should be written off rather than chased.
4. **Is the "near me" cluster winnable?** 826 impressions from inside Lithuania,
   positions 5–13, ~1 click. These compete with a local map pack. Worth
   establishing whether a directory page can ever take that click.
5. **The head terms need content and links**, which the owner has ruled out.
   Worth putting a number on it: what would page one for `וילנה` actually cost?

### Standing constraints

- **Never write or publish site content** — no blog posts, no place
  descriptions, no marketing copy.
- **Never push or deploy without explicit approval.**
- Technical and SEO work is free within those limits.
