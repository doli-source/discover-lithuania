Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Lithuania Discovery is committed to making this website accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities. We are working towards conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, as required by the European Accessibility Act (EAA).

Built-in Accessibility Widget

This website includes an accessibility widget — the amber button in the bottom corner of every page. It lets you:

  • Increase or decrease text size — use A+ and A− buttons
  • Enable high contrast mode — stronger colour contrast for low-vision users
  • Reduce motion — stops animations and transitions across the site

Your preferences are saved automatically for future visits.

Technical Standards

  • Semantic HTML5 elements throughout
  • ARIA labels on all interactive elements
  • Full keyboard navigation support
  • Colour contrast meeting WCAG AA (minimum 4.5:1 for body text)
  • Bilingual content in English and Hebrew (including RTL layout support)
  • Responsive design across all screen sizes and devices
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion system preference

Known Limitations

We are continuously improving accessibility across all pages. Some older static content pages may not yet fully meet every WCAG 2.1 AA criterion. We are actively working to address any gaps.
