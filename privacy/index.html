Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

General

Discover Lithuania (lithuaniadiscovery.com) is an independent travel guide run by Niv Shimoni. This page explains what data is collected from visitors and how it's used. The site doesn't require sign-up, and doesn't collect your name, email, or payment details through any form.

What data is collected

The site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are popular and how visitors use the site. This includes anonymous data such as: pages viewed, time on site, general geographic location (city/country), and device/browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies for this.

WhatsApp and external links

  • The floating WhatsApp button opens a direct chat with Niv in your own WhatsApp app. Anything you share there is covered by WhatsApp/Meta's privacy policy, not this site's.
  • Map links (Google Maps) and occasional links to local businesses or external blogs take you off this site. We don't control the privacy practices of those external sites.

Cookies

Aside from Google Analytics cookies, the site doesn't use additional tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Contact

Questions about your privacy on this site? Reach out directly: Niv.shimoni@gmail.com
