🏄 Indoor Surf Wave

citywave Vilnius

Vilnius, Lithuania

About this place

Lithuania's only indoor surf wave — a stationary wave machine that lets you surf, bodyboard, or stand-up paddle right in the middle of Vilnius. No ocean needed. Great for beginners and experienced surfers alike, with instructors available on site.
