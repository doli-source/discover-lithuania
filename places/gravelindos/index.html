Gravelindos

Vilnius, Lithuania

About this place

The first women's gravel cycling community in Lithuania — based in Vilnius and organizing free Thursday social rides called BIKE&WINE. Since 2023, Gravelindos has gathered over 3,000 women who come for the ride and stay for the laughs and desserts. If you're visiting Vilnius on a Thursday, this is your invitation.
