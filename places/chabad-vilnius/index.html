About

Chabad of Lithuania — the beating heart of Vilnius' small but proud Jewish community, led by Rabbi Sholom Ber Krinsky. If you're Jewish and traveling in Lithuania, this is your first stop: kosher home-style meals (order at least 48 hours ahead), Friday night Shabbat dinners, synagogue services, and a genuinely warm welcome. Call ahead before visiting — opening hours can be unpredictable.
